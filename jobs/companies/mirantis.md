# Mirantis: interview data from other candidates

Role on shortlist: Senior Full-Stack TypeScript Engineer, remote (Next.js App Router, Route Handlers, OIDC/JWT, Playwright, 8+ yrs ask).
Research date: 23 Nov 2026.

**Frontend/full-stack specific data: NOT found.** Mirantis is mostly a Kubernetes/cloud infra company, so public reviews are DevOps, PM and infra SWE roles. Pattern from those:

| Review | Role, place, date | Rounds | Questions | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Glassdoor | Senior DevOps, Berlin, Nov 2025 | HR screen (salary, notice) -> technical with 2 hiring managers -> VP (tech + behavioral) | Bare metal, Kubernetes experience | No offer; VP said not a match for **AI team**. ~3 months total, HR call came 2 months after applying |
| Glassdoor | Senior PM, Toronto, Aug 2025 | 1 HM interview, 45 min | Success criteria approach; working with sales/marketing; working with devs + UX | No offer |
| Glassdoor | Software Engineer, Manila, 2024 | 2 technical + behavioral | "Explain Kubernetes in your own words"; open-source contribution valued | Offer accepted, easy |
| Glassdoor | Software Engineer, New York, 2014 | Recruiter -> 3 hr tech with 2 engineers -> manager | KVM, routing, Puppet, bash, Python | Offer |

Salary: not on any page.

## Ayush ke liye
- Process slow ho sakta hai (2-3 mahine). Apply karke bhool jao, follow up karo.
- Likely rounds: HR (salary + notice period) -> 2 HMs ke saath technical -> VP.
- Role "AI team" ke liye ho sakta hai. Apna RAG assistant aur AI SDK kaam dikhana.
- Kubernetes ki basic samajh rakho ("explain in your own words"). Tumhara FITPASS ka standalone build + K8s health probe + Docker kaam yahan bolo.
- Job post mein OIDC/JWT aur Playwright hai: Day 9 aur Day 10 ka kaam isi ke liye.
- Open-source contribution ko value dete hain.
